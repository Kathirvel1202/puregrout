Service & Drying Times

Because our enclosed rotary equipment extracts virtually all water on contact, floors are only slightly damp immediately following service. Floors are generally dry to the touch in 1 to 2 hours with our industrial air movers. If a protective penetrative sealer is applied, we recommend waiting 4 hours before normal foot traffic and keeping moisture away for 24 hours.

Grout Care & Sealing

Grout is essentially colored concrete—it contains microscopic pores that act like an unpainted sponge. Without a penetrating sealer, dirty mop water, coffee spills, pet accidents, and cooking grease absorb directly into the core of the grout line where standard cleaning cannot reach. Sealing fills these microscopic voids with an invisible barrier that allows spills to bead on the surface for easy wiping.

Yes. If grease or dye has permanently penetrated an unsealed grout line, our Grout Color Sealing service applies a tinted pigment sealant that restores uniform color across every channel. For chipped or crumbling areas, we offer spot regrouting prior to deep cleaning.

For average residential households, professional cleaning once every 12 to 18 months keeps grout healthy and prevents permanent discoloration. For busy commercial properties, restaurants, medical clinics, or homes with multiple indoor pets, we recommend a semi-annual schedule.
