Our Story & Philosophy

Beyond Mops & Household Scrubbers.

Traditional mopping simply pushes dirty water and microscopic soils into porous grout channels. Over months and years, tiles turn dull and grout lines turn black.

PureGrout was established to provide homeowners and facility managers with a true commercial solution: heated water pressure containment that loosens soil, pulls it away instantly with high-velocity vacuum extraction, and locks in clean grout with breathable penetrative sealers.

Commercial Pressure Rotary Equipment

Our truck-mounted and high-performance portable extractors generate controlled water pressure inside an enclosed vacuum dome. This prevents messy overspray while thoroughly flushing porous grout cavities.

  • Enclosed rotary vacuum head prevents water splatter on baseboards and cabinets.
  • pH-balanced neutralization rinse prevents rapid resoiling.
  • Industrial air-movers accelerate drying so floors can be walked on in hours.
  • Clear penetrative sealants create an invisible barrier against grease and wine stains.
