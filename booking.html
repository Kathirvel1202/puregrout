Booking Assistance

          
Frequently Asked Questions About Booking

          
Everything you need to know about scheduling, technician arrival, and our cleaning process.

        


        

          

            
How far in advance should I book?

            
We recommend booking 2 to 4 days ahead for residential homes. For commercial properties needing after-hours or weekend service, scheduling 1 week in advance ensures your preferred time slot.

          

          

            
Do I need to move heavy furniture?

            
Our technicians can carefully move lightweight furniture like chairs, tables, and floor mats. For large entertainment centers or heavy appliances, we clean up to the baseboards unless pre-cleared.

          

          

            
How long does an appointment take?

            
Most residential projects (such as a kitchen and two bathrooms) take approximately 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Commercial dining rooms and lobbies vary by square footage.

          

          

            
Can I change or cancel my appointment?

            
Yes. You can reschedule or cancel at any time with at least 24 hours notice at no charge by contacting our dispatch team by phone or WhatsApp.
